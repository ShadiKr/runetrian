Runetrian is the written script of the language of magic in Potion in Motion, a game by Mystic Brew Games. In the game's lore, Runetrian was first inscribed with wands, in swift and precise strokes, and the magician Zara later standardized it into an alphabet that is quick to write. Every letter in this font is drawn that way: one or two thin swipes of a wand, laid on five shared guide lines, with nothing filled in.

Type English letters and they come out in Runetrian; upper and lower case write the same letters. Accented letters carry their accents as small swipes above or below the rune, and digits, punctuation and symbols are plain shapes drawn with the same wand stroke, so any text sets cleanly.

To contribute, see github.com/ShadiKr/runetrian.
